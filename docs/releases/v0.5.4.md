# Vibe Controller 0.5.4

## Saved defaults and controller feedback

- Fresh installs load the updated **GAPE (Gabe's defaults)** profile, including
  current system-wide shortcuts, LB/RB layers, Codex overrides, Share mappings,
  and per-button vibration patterns.
- Fresh-install vibration preferences match Gabe's setup: **enabled at 50%
  strength**, with the connection pulse enabled. Existing saved profiles and
  explicit vibration preferences are not overwritten on upgrade.
- Choose a vibration pattern in each button editor, preview it without firing
  the shortcut, or use the master mute/strength controls in the sidebar.
- The tested Xbox Series USB model (`045e:0b12`) uses direct native USB rumble;
  other supported connections use Apple's controller haptics interface.

## Experimental full USB input

- Adds a per-session **Enable full USB / Stop full USB** control for the tested
  Xbox Series USB model, plus input-report and Share-press counters.
- Reads complete USB reports and routes vibration through the same exclusive
  session. Existing mapping and Universal Control virtual mouse/keyboard output
  paths are retained; nothing needs installing on the other Macs.
- The signed session helper and pinned libusb dependency, source, and license
  are bundled. macOS administrator approval is required; no persistent helper
  or replacement driver is installed. This mode is **off on every launch**.
- Stops on quit, sleep, lost connection, or missed heartbeat; pulse durations
  are bounded and held inputs are released during cleanup.

**Known limitations:** full-session Share + vibration + cross-Mac behavior has
not yet completed its end-to-end physical-controller verification. Raw Share
capture and ordinary direct USB vibration were separately confirmed. After
leaving exclusive mode, macOS input can recover while vibration remains silent;
**unplug and reconnect the controller** if that happens. Other apps cannot use
the controller while full USB is active. Keep a trackpad available when testing.

## Local Demo Capture

- Hold Option while opening the app menu to reveal **Demo Capture…**.
- Opt-in, permission-gated local display recording, controller/action logs,
  sync slates, and a persistent recording indicator with Stop & Save.
- Optional follow-cursor review export for selected displays on the lead Mac,
  plus storage guardrails and explicit review of older takes.
- No automatic upload, microphone recording, or remote-Mac screen capture.

The release includes automated regression coverage for saved defaults, haptic
routing/cancellation, complete Share reports, exclusive-input priority, bounded
USB framing, and Demo Capture. Download the signed, notarized Apple-silicon DMG
below. Virtual Hardware Support is bundled in the app and also available as a
separate package for setup/recovery.
