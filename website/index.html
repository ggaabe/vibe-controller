    Connect & install.

    Connect your Xbox or PlayStation controller by USB or Bluetooth. Open the DMG and drag Vibe Controller to Applications.

    Allow Accessibility.

    Follow the app’s setup guide to approve Accessibility in System Settings. This lets your controller move the pointer and send shortcuts.

    Enable native handoff.

    Install the bundled Virtual Hardware Support and approve its Karabiner Driver Extension. The app checks each step as you go.

An administrator may need to approve the support installer. Nothing is silently installed, and your other Macs don’t need Vibe Controller. Read the setup guide ↗

A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

Good questions.

Ask one on GitHub ↗
Which controllers and Macs work?+

The packaged app runs on Apple-silicon Macs with macOS 14 or later. It supports Xbox-compatible controllers, PlayStation DualSense, and DualShock 4 through USB or Bluetooth. Available controls and vibration depend on the model and connection; some Xbox USB connections do not expose the Share button.

Do I need the app on every Mac?+

No, not for native Universal Control. Install Vibe Controller and the bundled virtual input support on the controller-connected Mac. Your other Macs use Apple’s Universal Control. Compatible devices must use the same Apple Account with two-factor authentication, with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Handoff enabled. Apple’s setup requirements.

Are dictation and OCR built in?+

Vibe Controller triggers your existing tools. Configure RT / R2 for your preferred dictation shortcut. For drag-to-copy text, we recommend buying TextSniper or using an equivalent OCR utility, then mapping X / Square to its capture shortcut. OCR software is separate and not included. Region screenshots use macOS’s built-in capture.

Do app-specific mappings follow me to another Mac?+

Keyboard output follows the Universal Control pointer, but the lead Mac cannot see which app is focused on the other Mac. App-specific mappings follow the foreground app on the lead Mac. Global mappings and controller modifier combinations are the predictable option across computers.

Is it free? Does it need an account?+

The beta is free to download, and Vibe Controller does not require its own account or subscription. Profiles stay on your Mac. Updates are distributed through GitHub; macOS Universal Control uses your Apple Account. You can inspect the source on GitHub and report issues there.

What happens when there’s an update?+

Installed release builds check GitHub daily, and you can use Check for Updates in the app. Update Now verifies the downloaded release and signing identity, updates the app in place, and relaunches it. Development builds remain separate and link to the releases page instead.
